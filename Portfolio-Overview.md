# Ronald Richardson

## Healthcare Cybersecurity Portfolio

**Governance, Risk & Compliance | Identity & Access Management**

My portfolio connects my background in behavioral health and clinical case management with my transition into healthcare cybersecurity. I focus on protecting sensitive information while keeping security requirements practical for the people delivering care. Through independent portfolio exercises, fictional healthcare scenarios, and hands-on labs, I show how I identify concerns, evaluate evidence, and explain their significance to patients, staff, and leadership. My goal is to help technical and business teams make informed decisions about risk and access.

Each repository demonstrates a separate skill. My healthcare risk assessment organizes twelve risks into five management priorities. Botium examines controls and evidence gaps; the fictional CogniScribe assessment evaluates an AI vendor’s approval conditions; and the vulnerability assessment connects technical observations to remediation and closure requirements. I built the MAT/MOUD web-based SaaS with assistance from agentic AI tools, then conducted a security self-assessment of authentication, permissions, tenant separation, and access logging. Its audit repository preserves reported remediation outcomes; the application remains a separate development project, with a mobile version in development. The completed Clinical Policy Review Suite evaluates five fictional healthcare policies for leadership. Supporting labs demonstrate investigation and detection skills.

Together, these projects demonstrate how I translate technical concerns into clear recommendations, accountable actions, and evidence requirements. I distinguish confirmed observations, missing evidence, historical test reports, and proposed improvements. Reviewing my own software is not an independent assurance engagement, and AI assistance does not establish the cause of a particular flaw. Neither written policies nor code changes alone prove compliance. I aim to bring clinical awareness, careful analysis, and clear communication to a healthcare GRC or IAM team, helping leaders understand what needs attention, who should act, and how improvement will be verified.
